# Project Manager — Job Description

**Type:** Full-time (Remote)  
**Mission:** Orchestrate intake ops, hiring pipeline, and cross-team delivery.

## Responsibilities
- Build hiring pipeline; coordinate interviews & onboarding
- Maintain SOPs & playbooks; enforce SLAs
- Run weekly ops standup; track risks & blockers
- Interface with partner law firms

## Requirements
- 3+ years ops or legal PM experience
- Process + metrics mindset
- Excellent written communication

## KPIs
- Time-to-fill openings
- SLA adherence across teams
- Stakeholder satisfaction
