# Intake Specialist — Job Description

**Type:** Full-time/Part-time (Remote)  
**Mission:** Be first contact for potential clients and convert to qualified intakes.

## Responsibilities
- Answer inbound calls/chats; conduct scripted pre-qual
- Schedule attorney/PM consults
- Track leads in CRM; ensure e-sign completion
- Weekly reporting on funnel metrics

## Requirements
- Call center or legal intake experience
- Excellent phone presence; bilingual a plus
- CRM/E-sign familiarity

## KPIs
- Lead-to-appointment set rate
- Show-up rate
- Signed-retainer conversion
