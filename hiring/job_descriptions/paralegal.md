# Paralegal (Mass Tort) — Job Description

**Type:** Full-time (Remote)  
**Mission:** Support mass-tort intake, medical record requests, and case file organization.

## Responsibilities
- Intake triage & eligibility checks (playbooks provided)
- Medical record retrieval & chronology updates
- Docket/calendar coordination
- Document prep: authorizations, requests, exhibits
- Maintain CRM accuracy

## Requirements
- 2+ years plaintiff-side experience (preferred mass tort)
- Strong writing & follow-through
- Tools: Google Workspace, CRM, e-sign

## KPIs
- Intake-to-qualified conversion rate
- SLA: record request turnaround
- Data completeness and error rate
